# Content Agent writing practice v1

This is an operator-authored training brief, not performance evidence.

Start with a specific reader question. Explain what happened using only supplied
sources, then explain why the question matters. Attribute news descriptions to
their publisher. A headline is not proof of details absent from its description.

Use short descriptive headings. Define unfamiliar terms at first use. Separate
observations, explanations, and hypothetical illustrations explicitly. Connect a
current event to an existing article where the connection helps the reader.

Prefer a concrete explanation of one mechanism over generic advice. Do not turn
uncertain evidence into predictions, investment recommendations, or invented
numbers. End with what remains unknown and what evidence would resolve it.

Practice each technique by writing a before/after example. The example must be
hypothetical, never a new factual claim. Assess the generated article against the
actual technique and quote where it was applied. Carry unresolved weaknesses
into the next practice session. A model's assessment is a fallible self-check,
not proof that readers prefer the result or that capability has improved.
