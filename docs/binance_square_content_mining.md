Write to Earn rewards attributed eligible reader trades, not post count or engagement. Base commission is 20%, weekly top 31-100 receive 30% total, top 1-30 receive 50%. Account KYC and regional availability must be confirmed. Eligible readers click a native coin tag/widget then order within one minute. A post can generate commission for seven days. CreatorPad rewards are mutually exclusive. Do not delete a post during the settlement week.

The FAQ describes manually published content; the official Square Skill separately supports automated text posts. API publication SUCCESS therefore does not prove mining eligibility or reward attribution. Account dashboard/actual per-post commission is required for acceptance. API-initiated reader trades are excluded; that exclusion is not itself proof about API-published content.
